Classroom++ Changelog

What's new and improved in Classroom++

  • v0.2.0

    October 2026 Current
    • NEWChalkboard Logo: Brand new icon inspired by Google Classroom with a green chalkboard, warm wood frame, and chalk "++".
    • NEWSettings Sync: Settings now automatically synchronize across all of your browsers and devices via Chrome Sync storage.
    • NEWExtension Modal & Onboarding: Full popup settings menu from the browser toolbar, welcome tour, and changelog.
    • FIXScroll Gap Elimination: Removed the trailing blank space at the bottom of topic scroll areas. Scrolling now cleanly stops flush with the final post.
    • FIXLoop Glitch Resolved: Completely resolved the infinite expanding/collapsing loop on long topics like Wednesday Write.
    • IMPROVEClean Drag-and-Drop: Removed unnecessary line feedback when dragging; drag controller isolated for smooth manual post reordering.

  • v0.1.1

    October 2026
    • IMPROVESmart Boundary Alignment: Improved alignment logic to place the latest published post in the center, next to upcoming drafts.
    • FIXHeader Menu Immunity: Protected kebab and dropdown menus from being mistaken for post lists.
    • FIXCreate Button Flash: Removed synthetic focus calls that caused the "+ Create" button to flash on page load.

  • v0.1.0

    October 2026
    • NEW5-Post Topic Window: Automatically caps topic sections to 5 visible posts with scroll and "View all" toggle.
    • NEWReverse Order: Added "Reverse order" button next to Google's "Collapse all" to sort posts in reverse.
