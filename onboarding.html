Welcome to Classroom++

By Jonathan Kung

Transform your Google Classroom Classwork feed into an organized, scrollable, and teacher-friendly workspace.

5-Post Scroll Windows

Every topic section is neatly capped to 5 visible posts with its own smooth scroll area. No more endless page scrolling!

Smart Draft & Post Centering

Automatically scrolls to the boundary where your latest published post meets upcoming drafts, so you can pick up editing right where you left off.

One-Click "View all" Toggle

Need to see everything in a topic at once? Click "View all" to expand the full topic feed, and "View fewer" to return to 5 posts.

Reverse Order & Device Sync

Toggle reverse order with one click in the header. Your settings sync automatically across all Chrome browsers you use.
